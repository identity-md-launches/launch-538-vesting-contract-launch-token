- forge-std **v1.9.7**: unmodified `src/` and the upstream MIT/Apache licenses.
  Source: https://github.com/foundry-rs/forge-std/tree/v1.9.7
  Release archive SHA-256: `45157353ab49eab01d294565866731e599b32401757229689ee459aa26b7ee94`.
